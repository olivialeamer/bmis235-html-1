Sew Cool!

My journey sewing and how you can start too!

I grew up sewing, learning from my grandma and making new projects every summer. When she passed away, I realized I missed sewing and found it to be a good way to connect with my grandma, while also having a creative outlet that challenged my mind.

Through the last bit of high school, I started making my own patterns and challenged myself to make different pieces that would test new skills and develop my knowledge of sewing.

When I moved to college, I struggled not having that same creative outlet, but I would sew when I went home for breaks. This year, I got a job at the GU Costume Shop for the Dance and Theatre department. Now I get to see the behind-the-scenes of the Gonzaga productions while also getting paid to do what I love.

Want to start sewing?

Try this out:

  • Find inspiration: Find a few simple ideas of where to start and look online for tutorials that can walk you through each step.
  • Start by hand-sewing: All you need is a needle, thread, and some fabric. It's a process of trial and error, but once you get the hang of it, you can start challenging yourself to develop new skills.
  • Upcycling is a great way to start: Thrift pieces of clothing that you would wear if they had a little tweak in them. This makes the process a lot less intimidating because you aren't starting from scratch.
