Olivia is Sew Cool!

A collection of my favorite sewing projects

I grew up sewing, learning from my grandma and making new projects every summer. When she passed away, I realized I missed sewing and found it as a good way to connect with her, while also having a creative outlet that challenged my mind.

Through the last bit of high school, I started making my own patterns and challenged myself to make different pieces that would test new skills and develop my knowledge of sewing.

When I moved to college, I struggled not having that same creative outlet, but I would sew when I went home for breaks. This year, I got a job at the GU Costume Shop for the Dance and Theatre department. Now I get to see the behind the scences of the Gonzaga productions while also getting paid to do what I love.

Below, I've created a collection of some of my favorite pieces. Feel free to take a peek!
